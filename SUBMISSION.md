# Nimsplit — Nimiq Pay Mini Apps Competition

**Tagline:** Split the bill. Settle in NIM.

Splitting a dinner should not require a spreadsheet, a bank app, and a week of reminders. People already know what they owe. The hard part is actually sending it — especially across wallets, currencies, and group chats.

Nimsplit is an instant group expense splitter that lives inside Nimiq Pay. Open the Mini App, join a group with a short code or QR, add the bill, and see exactly who owes whom. When it is your turn, tap Pay. Nimiq Pay opens a native confirmation. You approve. The share is settled.

There is no extra wallet setup, no custodial account, and no “we’ll mark it paid and hope.” NIM payments go through the official Nimiq Mini App provider. USDT payments go out on Polygon through the injected Ethereum provider. Nimsplit never holds funds and never asks for keys. A payment is settled only after the wallet actually submits the transaction.

That is why Nimiq Pay makes this better than a generic splitter. The wallet is already there. Confirmation is native. Settlement is instant enough for the end of a meal, not the end of the month.

The product is intentionally small: groups, expenses, equal or custom splits, balances, NIM and USDT payouts, status, history, and invite codes. Friday Dinner is seeded so anyone can join, see a 40 NIM bill, recognize a 10 NIM share, and pay it for real.

Nimsplit is for the last ten minutes of a night out — when the bill is on the table and nobody wants another app. Split the bill. Settle in NIM.
